## Product

The Enyrgy Vitamin D Primal Light Platform activates three biological pathways in 2 to 4 minutes: vitamin D synthesis (2.4x more efficient than sunlight), nitric oxide release, and serotonin production. The app reads each user's skin type, sets a personal dose, and ends the session automatically. It is a wellness device and makes no medical claims. Built in Phoenix, Arizona, Made in USA.

## Clinical and credibility

In a 12-week study, participants raised their average vitamin D from 39.96 to 84.20 ng/mL (a 111 percent increase), and 100 percent reached optimal levels. This was a small uncontrolled pilot (N = 5 healthy adults, no control group); larger trials are in development, and we cite it as a pilot, not a definitive result. Our Scientific Advisory Board includes Dr. Bruce Hollis (widely regarded as the Godfather of Vitamin D Research, 70,000+ citations), Dr. Samantha Kimball (designer of the dosing protocol behind every session), and Dr. William Grant (NASA UVB researcher).
