# ENYRGY - Brand Identity and Style Guide

**Sunlight. Evolved.**

Version 2.0 | June 2026 (project reference copy, Session 7) · Supersedes: Style Guide v6, September 2023

This is the governing brand authority for Enyrgy. All copy, design, and marketing assets follow it. No em-dashes anywhere. Age minimum resolved to 18 (see Section 08).

## 01 Brand Overview

### Brand Mission

Enyrgy exists to give people back the light their modern lives have taken away. We have engineered a precision phototherapy platform that delivers the biological benefits of midday sun (vitamin D synthesis, nitric oxide release, serotonin activation) in 2 to 4 minutes per day, indoors, year round, calibrated to each person's biology.

### Official Product Name

**Enyrgy Vitamin D Primal Light Platform**

This is the canonical product name. Use it in full on first reference in any document, webpage, or marketing asset. On subsequent references within the same piece, "the Platform" or "Enyrgy" is acceptable.

Avoid: "Precision Vitamin D Wellness Platform" or any other variant. These names appeared in earlier documents and are no longer canonical.

### Official Tagline

**"Sunlight. Evolved."**

Primary sub-tagline, used under the logo and in brand headline contexts. Always rendered in Bely Bold Italic (web fallback: Montserrat SemiBold Italic). The period after "Evolved" is intentional and required.

### Brand Positioning

Enyrgy is precision wellness technology: a clinically grounded, engineered product for serious buyers. State that positively. Do not define Enyrgy by what it is not, and never name a tanning device or tanning bed in customer-facing copy, even to deny the comparison. Naming it plants it. If a prospect raises the comparison themselves, answer it plainly using the objection script in the knowledge base. Every brand decision should reinforce that this is an engineered product for people who manage their biology on purpose. The positioning is peer-to-peer: operator speaking directly to a self-optimizing adult who tracks their labs, manages their biology intentionally, and does not need to be sold to. They need to be informed.

### Brand Essence in One Line

The intelligence of precision technology. The warmth of the sun. Zero compromise on either.

## 02 Logo Usage

The Enyrgy logo consists of the circular arc wordmark (the "e" with sun-arc) plus the ENYRGY logotype. It exists in two layouts and two colorways.

### Layouts

**Vertical (Primary).** Mark stacked above logotype. Use for hero placements, standalone brand moments, product labeling, print collateral, social profile images.

**Horizontal (Secondary).** Mark to the left of logotype. Use for website navigation and header bar, email headers, co-branded materials where vertical space is limited.

### Color Variations

**Full Color (Primary).** Sunrise Orange mark plus logotype on white or Calm Beige background. Default for all digital and print applications.

**Reversed (Secondary).** White mark plus logotype on Sunrise Orange background. Use for orange-background sections, product box, apparel, promotional items.

**Single-Color Dark.** Charcoal (#1A1A1A) mark plus logotype on light backgrounds. Use when brand color is not available (newspapers, legal documents, embroidery, laser engraving).

**Single-Color White.** White mark plus logotype on any dark or photographic background.

### Clear Space and Minimum Size

Maintain clear space equal to the cap-height of the "E" on all sides. Never crowd the logo with other text or graphic elements. Minimum size: 1.25 inches / 200px wide. Do not reproduce the logo smaller than this.

### Logo Don'ts

Do not recolor the logo outside the approved variations. Do not rotate, skew, stretch, or distort it. Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or effects. Do not place the full-color logo on a busy photographic background (use white or single-color instead). Do not recreate the logo in a different font. Do not use the wordmark without the arc mark.

## 03 Color Palette

The Enyrgy palette is built on two primary brand colors plus a supporting neutral system for digital applications. Both primary colors are unchanged from v1.

### Primary Brand Colors

| Color | Hex | Pantone | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|---------|-----|-------|
| Sunrise Orange | #E64C38 | P 45-6 U | 230 / 76 / 56 | CTAs, buttons, key stats, accent lines, logo, orange-field sections |
| Calm Beige | #ECE4CF | P 8-9 U | 236 / 228 / 207 | Section backgrounds, cards, tinted panels, print collateral body areas |

### Supporting Colors (New in v2.0)

Added for digital UI. They do not replace the primary colors, they work alongside them.

| Color | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Deep Charcoal | #1A1A1A | 26 / 26 / 26 | Dark-section backgrounds, body text on light backgrounds, footer areas |
| Pure White | #FFFFFF | 255 / 255 / 255 | Primary page background, white-field sections, reversed text |
| Warm Off-White | #F7F4EE | 247 / 244 / 238 | Subtle section differentiation, warmer alternative to pure white |

### Color Application Ratios

**Print / Packaging (unchanged from v1):** 80% Sunrise Orange, 20% Calm Beige. Orange is dominant where brand recognition is the objective.

**Digital / Website (updated in v2.0):** the ratio inverts. White and Warm Off-White carry 60 to 70% of the page area. Calm Beige sections add warmth at 15 to 20%. Sunrise Orange is the power accent: buttons, key statistics, section markers, underlines, and CTAs. Charcoal handles dark hero sections and the footer.

Why the change: a $2,995 precision technology product needs white space to signal premium and clinical credibility. Orange-dominant digital layouts read as consumer wellness, not precision technology. The color is unchanged, only its web application ratio has evolved.

### Color Don'ts

Do not introduce colors outside this palette without brand approval. Do not place Sunrise Orange text on Calm Beige backgrounds (contrast is insufficient for accessibility). Do not use gradients or tints of the primary brand colors. Do not use Sunrise Orange for large background areas on the website (use it sparingly and intentionally for impact).

## 04 Typography

Enyrgy uses a two-font system: Montserrat as the primary brand typeface for all headlines, body copy, UI text, and navigation; Bely Bold Italic as the editorial accent for subheads and callouts. Together they balance precision (Montserrat's clean geometry) with warmth and distinction (Bely's editorial personality).

Note on Montserrat vs Red Hat Display: Montserrat replaces Red Hat Display as the primary brand typeface in v2.0, for its wider weight range (9 weights, Thin to Black), superior digital hinting, broader system availability, and stronger support for data-heavy layouts. It is free on Google Fonts and requires no license for web or print.

### Primary Typeface: Montserrat

Google Fonts, free, no licensing required. Download: fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat

**Typography scale, web (px):**

| Level | Size | Example |
|-------|------|---------|
| Hero H1 | 52 | Precision phototherapy. |
| Page H1 | 40 | The way nature intended. |
| Section H2 | 28 | Triple-Pathway Advantage. |
| Subsection H3 | 22 | Vitamin D Synthesis (UVB) |
| Body Regular | 16 | Your body creates vitamin D. It does not absorb it. |
| Body Bold | 16 Bold | Average baseline: 39.96 ng/mL |
| Label / Caption | 12 SemiBold | TRIPLE-PATHWAY ADVANTAGE |
| Button / CTA | 14 Bold | Get Yours |

**Typography scale, print (pt):** Headline 34 pt; Subhead (print body) 16 pt; Body 10 pt / 14 pt lead.

### Weight Hierarchy

Black (900): largest hero statements only, sparingly. ExtraBold (800): hero headline weight for most H1. Bold (700): section headings (H2), CTAs, buttons, bold callout stats. SemiBold (600): H3 subheadings, labels, navigation. Regular (400): all body copy. Light (300): pull quotes in large-type contexts only. Do not use Thin (100) or ExtraLight (200) in brand contexts.

### Editorial Accent Typeface: Bely

Adobe Fonts, requires Adobe Creative Cloud subscription. Confirm developer access before project launch. Used for subheads in print and editorial contexts, the "Sunlight. Evolved." tagline, and callout quotes. Always Bely Bold Italic, no other variant. Web fallback when Bely is unavailable: Montserrat SemiBold Italic.

### Typography Don'ts

No more than two typefaces in any single piece. Do not use Bely for body copy or UI (editorial accent only). Do not set body copy below 10pt (print) or 15px (web). Do not use all-caps for body paragraphs (reserve caps for labels and category tags). Do not mix font weights arbitrarily. Do not use Bely Bold Italic on mobile body copy below 18px (use Montserrat SemiBold Italic instead).

## 05 Voice and Tone

### Who We Are Talking To

The Enyrgy buyer is a self-directed, analytically minded adult. They research before they buy. They track their labs. They have already invested in their health (red light therapy panels, continuous glucose monitors, cold plunge systems). They have read the literature or know it exists. They are not impressed by vague wellness language. Speak to them as a knowledgeable peer, not a brand talking at a customer.

### Voice Principles (Updated v2.0)

| Principle | We are... | We are not... |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Direct | Specific, data-grounded, peer-to-peer. We cite the study, name the number, state the mechanism. | Vague, hedged, or aspirational without evidence. |
| Precise | Exact language over approximate. "+111% from baseline" over "dramatic results." | Hyperbolic. We do not say "revolutionary," "game-changing," or "transforms." |
| Transparent | We show limitations. We name what we are not. We describe contraindications without minimizing them. | Evasive. We do not hide behind FDA wellness language to avoid hard questions. |
| Confident without ego | We state what we know clearly. We acknowledge what we do not know. | Arrogant. We do not dismiss alternatives or attack competitors by name. |
| Warm but not soft | Human, grounded, founder-voiced. We write in full sentences, not bullet spray. | Corporate, overly formal, or performed. We do not say "empower" or "journey." |
| Calibrated | We match the register to the context. Product page is tighter than the Science page. Email is conversational. Ads are sharp. | Consistent to a fault. We do not write every piece in the same voice regardless of context. |

### Voice Examples

**Weak (old voice):** "Enyrgy helps you feel your best with the healing power of light. Safe for the body. Healing for the soul."

**Strong (Enyrgy voice):** "Your body creates vitamin D. It does not absorb it. When UVB hits your skin, you synthesize D3, vitamin D sulfate, and photoproducts no supplement contains. That is the mechanism. That is why supplements still leave 40% of users deficient."

**Weak:** "Experience the transformative power of sunlight, reimagined for your modern lifestyle."

**Strong:** "600+ users. 24,000+ sessions. 0% return rate. Average vitamin D increase: 111%. That is what the data shows."

### Mechanics

## 06 Imagery Direction

Enyrgy imagery must work harder than generic wellness stock photography. The buyer is analytical and skeptical. Every image should either (a) show the product in context, (b) establish scientific credibility, or (c) show a real, specific human engaged in the Enyrgy protocol, not a generic wellness moment.

**Category 1: Product / Device.** The Platform is the hero. Prioritize clean backgrounds (white, Warm Off-White, or Charcoal), precision lighting that captures device detail, lifestyle-in-use shots showing correct session distance and posture, and multi-user household contexts showing the 6-user value. Avoid outdoors settings, product floating in abstract space, and generic glowing-light effects that obscure device detail.

**Category 2: Active / Performance.** Athletes, performance-focused individuals, post-workout recovery. Images should feel disciplined and intentional, not a beach vacation. Think pre-race preparation, early-morning training, strength and conditioning. Skin exposure is appropriate and necessary (the product requires skin access). Tone: focused, physical, driven. Avoid extreme sport spectacle, injury imagery, and beach or leisure contexts that undermine the performance positioning.

**Category 3: Scientific / Clinical (New in v2.0).** Required for the Science page, advisory board sections, white paper design, and any content targeting the Stack Optimizer ICP. Includes advisory board headshots in professional settings, lab environments (clean, modern, not sterile and cold), data visualization aesthetics, macro imagery of skin and light interaction, UV spectrum diagrams. Tone: precise, credible, peer-reviewed. Avoid stock-photo doctors in white coats with stethoscopes and clinical imagery that feels medical-device rather than wellness-technology.

**Category 4: Lifestyle / Wellness.** Supporting category for editorial, email, and social. People enjoying natural light, outdoors moments, family health contexts. Warm, golden light tones. Subordinate to product and science imagery on the website. Avoid the current website hero (generic wheat-field stock); replace with product-in-use or a founder moment.

**Photography style:** warm, directional natural light (avoid flat studio lighting). Warm whites and golds. Represent Fitzpatrick skin types III to VI prominently (the safety and efficacy story is skin-type specific and diverse representation is accurate product representation). No forced smiles or generic poses. Aspect ratios: 16:9 website hero, 1:1 social, 4:5 Instagram portrait.

## 07 Naming and Trademark Guidelines

### Official Product Name

**Enyrgy Vitamin D Primal Light Platform.** Use this exact name, in full, on first reference in every document, webpage, marketing asset, and PR mention. Subsequent references in the same piece: "the Platform," "the Enyrgy Platform," or "Enyrgy."

Deprecated names (do not use in any new materials): "Precision Vitamin D Wellness Platform" and any variant of "BioCalibrated Sunshine Platform."

### Brand Name

"Enyrgy," always with a capital E, never all-caps (ENYRGY) outside of the logo lockup itself.

### Tagline

"Sunlight. Evolved." Both words capitalized. Both periods required. Do not alter punctuation. Used directly beneath the logo (vertical layout), in editorial headlines, and in brand advertising.

### Trademark Terms

**Triple-Pathway Advantage(TM).** Always hyphenated, always title-cased, include the trademark symbol on first mention in any document or page. Describes the three simultaneous biological pathways triggered by one Enyrgy session: vitamin D synthesis (UVB), nitric oxide release (UVA), serotonin activation (UVA).

**BioCalibrated Sunshine(TM).** A secondary descriptive trademark for the Enyrgy method of precision-dosed, skin-type-calibrated phototherapy. Use sparingly, primarily in white papers, editorial, and PR. Not recommended as primary product copy on the website. Include the trademark symbol on first mention.

**MED Protocol.** "MED" stands for Minimal Erythemal Dose. Do not trademark this term, it is a scientific standard. Use it with the definition on first mention in any consumer-facing material: "Minimal Erythemal Dose (MED), the precise amount of UVB exposure that triggers vitamin D synthesis without causing a burn."

## 08 Legal and Compliance

**Age requirement: 18.** Resolved Session 7. The minimum age is 18 years or older, consistent with the current website, all website copy, both white papers, and comparable consumer wellness devices (red light therapy, infrared saunas, phototherapy panels). The earlier v1 "21 years or older" language is retired. Update any asset that still shows 21.

### Required Disclosures

All consumer-facing materials must include the following. Exact placement and format vary by medium.

**Age Restriction.** Not suitable for use by individuals under 18 years of age.

**Medical Device / FDA Status.** Enyrgy is not a medical device. Enyrgy does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Statements have not been evaluated by the FDA.

**Contraindication Notice.** Do not use if you have photosensitivity disorders, a personal or family history of melanoma or skin cancer, are taking photosensitizing medications, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under 18. Consult your physician before use if you have any medical condition or take any medications.

**Annual Skin Exam Recommendation.** Annual dermatological skin examinations are recommended for all users.

### Research Citation Guidelines

When citing peer-reviewed studies: state the specific finding, name the publication, and include the year. Example: "Narrowband UVB 3x/week produced greater vitamin D increases than 1,600 IU/day oral D3, British Journal of Dermatology RCT, 2012." For population-level epidemiological findings, frame as "research shows that adequate vitamin D levels are associated with...", never as a claim about what Enyrgy prevents or treats. Draw all research claims from published literature. Do not extrapolate beyond what the cited studies demonstrate.

## 09 Digital Application Guidelines

New in v2.0. The original guide was print-centric.

### Website Color System

Primary background: #FFFFFF (Pure White), default for all main content. Warm content sections: #F7F4EE (Warm Off-White), alternating section backgrounds, testimonial and editorial panels. Brand accent sections: #E64C38 (Sunrise Orange), hero sections, CTA blocks, dividers, max 2 to 3 per page. Dark sections: #1A1A1A (Deep Charcoal), footer, dark hero variants, proof and stats callouts. Beige panels: #ECE4CF (Calm Beige), in-page callout boxes, card backgrounds, pull-quote backgrounds.

### Button and CTA Hierarchy

Primary button: Sunrise Orange background plus White text (Montserrat Bold 14px). The single most important action on a page (for example, "Get Yours"). Secondary button: White background plus Sunrise Orange border plus Sunrise Orange text (for example, "Read the Science"). Ghost button: transparent background plus White border plus White text, on orange or dark backgrounds only.

### Typography, Web Specs

Montserrat from Google Fonts CDN. Import only weights in use: 300, 400, 600, 700, 800. Bely via Adobe Fonts (Typekit), requires active Creative Cloud license on the hosting account. Web fallback stack when brand fonts fail: Montserrat, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif.

### Accessibility Minimums

Minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1 for body text (WCAG AA). Orange (#E64C38) on White is 3.8:1 and does NOT meet AA for body text; use Charcoal on White for body. Orange is approved for large text (24px+) and UI elements only. White on Orange is 3.8:1, approved for large headlines and button text (minimum 18px bold). Charcoal on White is 18.1:1, fully compliant. Charcoal on Beige is 11.4:1, fully compliant.

### Social Media

Primary account: Instagram @enyrgy.light (primary and active). Ensure all website footer links, bio links, and marketing materials point to @enyrgy.light; audit and redirect any deprecated handles. Profile image: the logo mark (circular arc symbol only, no logotype) on Sunrise Orange, minimum 400x400px (upload at 1080x1080px). Content formats: feed 1:1 (1080x1080) or 4:5 (1080x1350), product-in-use and science content; stories and reels 9:16 (1080x1920), lifestyle and educational. Maintain brand color, Montserrat, and voice in all caption copy.
