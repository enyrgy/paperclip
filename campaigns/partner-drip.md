## Why the two claim changes mattered here more than elsewhere

**"World's first precision phototherapy platform"** was not in the KB and was not verifiable as written. Narrowband UVB phototherapy has existed in dermatology since the 1980s, so anyone wanting to dispute it only had to point at a clinical unit and argue category definitions in the middle of a partnership conversation. The claim itself is sound; it needed to name what is actually new. Per-user app calibration, per-skin-type MED and an automatic cutoff have no prior art, so the revised form says first and cannot be knocked down.

**"Scaling now with paid channels"** sat four lines below "Zero paid advertising to date" in the same email, and paid acquisition is deferred in the Master TODO until domain warmup completes. Partners run diligence. An email arguing with itself is a worse signal than the growth number it was trying to inflate.

**On the N=5 disclosure:** partners are the audience most likely to open the study, and they are deciding whether to put their own brand on the device. Discovering the denominator themselves after an email led with "100%" is a materially different experience from being told. The three drips now handle this by audience: Commercial and Partner state it in the copy, Consumer relies on the science page.
