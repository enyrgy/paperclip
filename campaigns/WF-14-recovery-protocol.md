## Touch 4B carried the flagged phrase verbatim

The Session 14 audit described the fault as emails that *"tied the 30-day money-back window to an 8-to-12-week lab retest (**'risk only a lab fee'**)."*

Touch 4B said, in the same paragraph as the retest instruction: *"The thirty-day money-back guarantee means the only thing you really risk is the lab fee."*

Not a variation. The flagged construction, still live.

An athlete following that instruction retests at week ten and finds the window closed six weeks earlier. That is a customer who did exactly what the email told them to do, which is worse than one who never bought.

**Fifth and sixth instances**, across four consecutive workflows: WF-17 touches 4 and 7, WF-13 touch 5, WF-16 touch 3A, and now both arms here.
