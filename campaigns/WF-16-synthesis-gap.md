## The A/B divergence, which is the finding worth keeping

**Touch 3B carried the corrected guarantee language. Touch 3A did not.**

The Session 14 audit decoupled the 30-day window from the eight-to-twelve-week lab retest and recorded it as done everywhere. In this workflow it reached one variant of one touch and stopped.

That is how it survived. Anyone spot-checking WF-16 would open a touch, find 3B's correct version, and move on satisfied. The uncorrected copy was sitting in the sibling variant the whole time.

**This is the fourth instance of the same coupling**, after WF-17 touches 4 and 7 and WF-13 touch 5. It has now been found in three consecutive workflows after being declared fixed everywhere.

**The operational lesson:** an A/B workflow needs both arms checked. A fix applied to one variant looks identical to a fix applied to the workflow.

## Both nitric oxide passages are compliant, and worth reading before writing any more

The KB forbids "no supplement produces nitric oxide". Both 2A and 2B avoid it correctly by granting that arginine and citrulline feed the enzymatic route, then confining the claim to photorelease, which no oral supplement performs. 2B goes further with "It is additive to anything you already take, not a replacement for it."

That is the framing to copy the next time this pathway comes up anywhere.
